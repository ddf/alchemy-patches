# CONDOLENCES

### STEREO SPECTRAL PROCESSOR INSPIRED BY SYMPATHETIC VIBRATIONS

Your signal will be analyzed with varying degrees of attention to ascertain the frequency of the vibrations contained therein, we will then introduce our own interpretations when resythesizing your input. You may mix your signal with ours to produce a novel perspective that is greater than the sum of its parts.

**Condolences** is two [phase vocoders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase_vocoder), one per stereo channel, with an analysis frame size of 1024 samples and a synthesis frame size of 4096 samples. Every 1024 samples, the input signal is analyzed and transferred to the synthesis spectrum, which is then resynthesized to produce the output. Spectral information is modified during both the transfer from analysis to synthesis, as well as prior to synthesizing a frame. The parameters on the Vibe and Rizz pages give the performer control over this modification process.

 Use **Perception** to determine how much of the input spectrum you want to pay attention to and **Focus** to thicken its impact on the synthesis spectrum. **Empathy** will determine how sensitive the synthesis spectrum is to the analysis, while **Sympathy** extends the decay time of frequencies we resonate with. You may also **Transpose** and **Warp** the frequency content of your signal before it is transferred for synthesis. The synthesized spectrum can be further animated using **Melt**, **Smear**, **Sizzle** and **Emote**. The result can be quite psychedelic and experiementation is important here. All parameters can be 'skewed' so that the value used for the left and right processors are pushed in opposite directions from the parameter value.
 
 By default, Condolences runs the phase vocoders in parallel. Two additional modes are available which either sum the input to a mono signal before parallel processing, or processes that mono signal in series.
